Morality-as-Cooperation (MAC) dimensions:
- Family: Kin selection, duty of care, special obligations to kin, familial loyalty vs neglect and incest.
- Group: Coordination, mutualism, loyalty, unity, solidarity, conformity vs betrayal and treason.
- Reciprocity: Reciprocal altruism, trustworthiness, forgiveness vs cheating and ingratitude.
- Heroism: Conflict resolution in contests, bravery, fortitude, largesse vs cowardice and miserliness.
- Deference: Conflict resolution in contests, respect, obedience, humility vs disrespect and hubris.
- Fairness: Conflict resolution in bargaining, impartiality, equality, fair division vs unfairness and favoritism.
- Property: Conflict resolution over possession, respect for ownership rights vs theft and trespass.
