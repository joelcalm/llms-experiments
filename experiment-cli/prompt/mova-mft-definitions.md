Moral Foundations Theory categories:
- Care/Harm: Compassion, kindness, protection from suffering; vs cruelty, violence, injury.
- Fairness/Cheating: Justice, rights, equality, proportionality, honesty; vs fraud, deception, exploitation.
- Loyalty/Betrayal: Allegiance, patriotism, group solidarity; vs treason, desertion, backstabbing.
- Authority/Subversion: Respect for legitimate hierarchy, duty, leadership, obedience; vs rebellion, disrespect, disorder.
- Sanctity/Degradation: Purity, sacredness, chastity, elevated living; vs contamination, pollution, desecration.
